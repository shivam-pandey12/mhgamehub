Combat Controls

Everything important is grouped by system so the move set is easier to learn before you enter the fight.
MovementLeft / Right walk, Shift runs, Up jumps.
Light CombatTap A quick slash, Hold A omni slash combo, Up + A launcher, Air + Down + A slam, moving Shift + A dash attack.
Heavy CombatTap D spin, Hold D charged strike, Air D heavy slam.
Energy TimingTap F quick slash, hold and release for energy slash or teleport slash.
Blink And SuperTap Y blink directly in front of the enemy, Left/Right + B lands a super slash that sends the target to that arena edge.
DefenseTap S parry, Hold S guard.
Mobile TouchOn phones, use the left joystick for movement, the separate run button for sprinting, and the right cluster for Light, Heavy, Energy, Guard, Super, and Blink inputs.
