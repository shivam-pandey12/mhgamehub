How to Play NeoTacTix

Basic Rules

NeoTacTix is an enhanced version of Tic-Tac-Toe where players take turns placing their marks on a grid.

The first player to get their marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins!

For boards larger than 3x3, you need 4 in a row to win.

Game Modes

  • Classic: Traditional Tic-Tac-Toe rules
  • Timed: You have 5 seconds to make each move
  • Power-Up: Use special abilities once per game
  • Fog of War: Limited visibility around your last move
  • Battle: Each mark has health that decreases when near enemies
  • Elemental: Choose elements with unique interactions

Power-Ups

  • Swap: Exchange one of your marks with an opponent's
  • Extra Turn: Make two moves in a row
  • Erase: Remove an opponent's mark from the board
  • Block: Place an unbreakable wall in a cell

Each power-up can only be used once per game per player.

Advanced Features

  • 3D Mode: View the board in 3D perspective
  • Themes: Change the visual style of the game
  • Save/Load: Continue your games later
  • Element Interactions:
    • Fire beats Earth, loses to Water
    • Water beats Fire, loses to Electric
    • Earth beats Air, loses to Fire
    • Air beats Electric, loses to Earth
    • Electric beats Water, loses to Air
