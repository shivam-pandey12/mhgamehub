How to Play

Core controls

  • Click or press space or up to boost the mech.
  • Double-click or press control to shoot.
  • Press 1 to 4 to activate powerups.
  • Press P or Escape to pause the run.

Powerup roles

  • T: Time Warp slows the whole field.
  • Q: Quantum Phase lets you pass through hazards.
  • G: Gravity Pulse flips your control rhythm for a short burst.
  • O: Overdrive grants immunity and rapid fire.
